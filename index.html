UHV Core Concepts & Reference Guide

Comprehensive study material based on Universal Human Values (BSH23VE01) curriculum.

🌐 1. The 4 Levels of Living (Samagra Vyavastha) ▼

Living in harmony requires understanding existence at four interdependent levels:

  • 1. Individual (Self & Body): Harmony between 'I' (Consciousness/Information) and 'Body' (Material tool).
  • 2. Family (Parivar): Living in harmony with immediate relationships based on mutual trust and respect.
  • 3. Society (Samaj): Extending harmony to extended community, work, and institutional structures.
  • 4. Nature / Existence (Prakriti / Sah-astitva): Living in balance with soil, water, plants, animals, and cosmic order.
🤝 2. Nine Foundational Feelings in Relationships ▼

Relationships are not based on physical contracts but on internal values (*Bhāv*):

1. Trust (Vishwas) Foundation of all relationship. Assurance that the other intends my happiness.
2. Respect (Samman) Right evaluation of the other person without differentiation or ego.
3. Affection (Sneha) Acceptance of the other person as one's relative or equal partner.
4. Care (Mamata) Responsibility & commitment to nurture and protect the physical body.
5. Guidance (Vatsalya) Responsibility & commitment to ensure Right Understanding in others.
6. Gratitude (Krutagyata) Feeling of acceptance for those who contributed to my growth.
7. Glory (Gaurav) Feeling of honour for those who made effort for excellence.
8. Reverence (Shraddha) Feeling of acceptance for those who have achieved Right Understanding.
9. Love (Prem) Complete feeling of oneness with every single human being.
🌳 3. The 4 Orders of Nature & Mutual Fulfillment ▼

1. Material Order (Padartha Avastha): Soil, water, air, metals. (Activity: Composition / Decomposition)

2. Bio / Plant Order (Prana Avastha): Plants, trees, greenery. (Activity: Respiration & Growth)

3. Animal Order (Jana Avastha): Animals & birds. (Activity: Selection & Desire to survive)

4. Human Order (Gyan Avastha): Humans. (Activity: Desire for Happiness, Right Understanding, Knowledge)

💡 Mutual Fulfillment (Paraspar Purnata): Orders 1, 2, and 3 naturally enrich each other. Humans currently exploit all three orders. Right understanding helps humans become mutually fulfilling to all orders.
⚖️ 4. Natural Acceptance (Sahaj Swvikriti) vs Pre-conditioning ▼

Natural Acceptance: The innate human capacity to know what is naturally acceptable without doubts, peer pressure, or external enforcement (e.g., wanting trust over mistrust, love over hatred).

Pre-conditioning (Manya): Unexamined beliefs absorbed from social media, peer culture, or media advertisements without self-verification.

Samyam (Self-Control) & Swasthya (Health): Feeling of responsibility toward the body to keep it nurtured, protected, and properly utilized.
